# Apex Studio brand policy

**Effective date: 8 November 2026**

This policy covers the Apex Studio name, wordmark, logo, application icon and other artwork created to identify Apex Studio (the “Apex Marks”). The Apex Marks are not licensed under the repository's Apache License 2.0. All rights in them remain with their respective owners.

## Allowed use with Apex Studio

You may reproduce the Apex Marks as they are included in this repository only to identify the official Apex Studio application or a modified build made from this project's source. If you distribute a modified build while keeping any Apex Marks, you must:

1. Clearly label it as an unofficial, modified fork, near the product name and in its release page.
2. Not state or imply that the build is official, supported, approved, or affiliated with the Apex Studio maintainers.
3. Preserve the marks without suggesting that changes to the software were made by the Apex Studio maintainers.

Fork maintainers are encouraged to retain Apex Studio attribution where practical. They may remove or replace all Apex Marks and use a distinct product name; the Apache-licensed code does not require use of the Apex Marks.

## Uses that need written permission

Written permission from the Apex Studio rights holder is required to:

- Use an Apex Mark as the name, logo, icon, domain or identity of an unrelated app, product, service or organization.
- Use an Apex Mark in a way that implies endorsement, sponsorship, official status or affiliation beyond identifying the original project or an unofficial fork as allowed above.
- Modify or combine an Apex Mark to create a new product identity, or use an Apex Mark in advertising unrelated to the project.

Nothing in this policy grants ownership of an Apex Mark or permission to register it, or a confusingly similar mark, as a product or organization identity. Third-party trademarks remain subject to their owners' terms.

## Code and other files

The Apache License 2.0 applies to the project's original application source code. It does not grant rights in Apex Marks. In particular, brand artwork in `design/`, `apps/desktop/public/brand/`, and `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` is excluded from the code licence. Fonts and other third-party files remain under their own licences and notices.

This policy does not narrow the rights granted for code by Apache-2.0. A person may use, modify, build, redistribute, or commercially use the code under that licence, including after removing the Apex Marks.

For written brand permission, contact the Apex Studio maintainers through the [Apex Studio Discord](https://discord.gg/VPMyGVEu3j). Permission must be explicit; a request or Discord membership alone is not permission.
